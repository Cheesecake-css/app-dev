# Avengers: Endgame (2019)

![Avengers Endgame](https://image.tmdb.org/t/p/w500/orig.jpg)

## 🎬 Movie Information

- **Title:** Avengers: Endgame
- **Release Year:** 2019
- **Directors:** Anthony Russo and Joe Russo
- **Genre:** Action, Adventure, Science Fiction
- **Production Company:** Marvel Studios
- **Based on:** Marvel Comics

## 📖 Synopsis

After the devastating events of *Avengers: Infinity War*, the remaining Avengers must find a way to reverse the destruction caused by Thanos.

With the help of time travel, they attempt to recover the Infinity Stones and restore balance to the universe.
